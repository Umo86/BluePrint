# Reports and the organiser overview

## Show overview

Each show's **Overview** page shows:

- sign-ups, checklist completion (required tasks done across every stand) and days to build-up;
- **Needs attention**: stands with overdue tasks, forms waiting for review, space-only packs to review, invitations not sent and emails that bounced in the last week (each links to the filtered list);
- **Deadlines** in the next 30 days (and the last week) with how many stands have done each;
- **Stands to chase**: the stands with the most overdue tasks, with an "Email them" button;
- shop totals for people with order or report rights.

Super admins see every show's sign-ups, checklist completion, stands behind and shop sales on the super admin overview.

## Exhibitors list

Filter by sign-up status, stand type, hall, checklist progress (behind, waiting for review, changes asked for, not started, all done) or a task not done. Tick companies to **email them** (an email draft for just those stands, which you write and send from Emails) or **download them** to Excel. "Download this list" downloads whatever the filters show.

Each exhibitor's record shows its checklist (every task, its deadline and status) and its orders.

On a task's **Responses** tab, "Email the stands that have not done it" drafts a reminder to exactly those stands.

## Excel reports

**Reports** in a show's menu. Each file has an "About this file" sheet saying what it is, when it was downloaded, by whom and with which filters.

| Report | Contents | Who can download |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exhibitors | Companies, stands and their sizes, main contact, sign-up, checklist progress, orders and spend | Manage exhibitors or view reports |
| Exhibitor contacts | Everyone with portal access, their role, last sign-in and whether they unsubscribed from updates | Manage exhibitors or view reports |
| Checklist by stand | One row per stand, one column per task | Manage exhibitors or view reports |
| All form answers | One sheet per form with every answer (or one form, from its Responses tab) | Manage exhibitors or view reports |
| Orders | Orders and order items (with product codes) with VAT, refunds, commission and payouts | Manage orders or view reports |
| Services by stand | The build-up delivery list, by hall and stand (with product codes, options and uploaded artwork names), and totals by product | Manage orders or view reports |
| Commission and payouts | Per supplier | Manage orders or view reports |
| Space-only approvals | Stand width, depth, area and open sides, contractor, pack status, insurance, decision and certificate | Review space-only, manage exhibitors or view reports |

### How the files are kept safe

- Every download checks the person's rights for that show and is written to the audit log (report, number of rows, filters).
- Each person can download up to 60 reports an hour.
- Cells are written as plain text, numbers or dates, never formulas, so nothing an exhibitor typed can run when the file is opened.
- Times are shown in UK time.
- The files contain personal information: keep them within Media10 and delete them when no longer needed.
